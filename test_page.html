🏮 Step 4: Cultural Byte
💡 YOKOCHO HISTORY: The "Tare" Legacy
These cramped post-WWII alleyways (Yokocho) originally operated as survival black markets. Vendors used a thick, sugary soy glaze called "Tare" to heavily mask and enrich cheaper, resource-scarce cuts of meat for working-class citizens. Today, ordering items with "Tare" preserves that rich history.
