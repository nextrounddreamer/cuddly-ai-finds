Bilingual Concept Guide / 双语核心概念

English Concept English Explanation 中文解析
Untied Loan Guarantee (ULG) A credit guarantee provided by an ECA or state institution that does not restrict the purchase of goods or services to the issuing country, allowing flexible blended capital structuring. 无绑定贷款担保
Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Credit Cover Specialized trade credit and political risk insurance designed to de-risk overseas battery storage installations against counterparty default or grid integration risks. 电池储能系统信用保险
Co-Insurance Framework A joint risk-sharing arrangement between two or more Export Credit Agencies to insure multi-sourcing international projects under unified documentation. 联合保险/共保机制
Standby Letter of Credit (SBLC) A legal document issued by a bank guaranteeing payment to a seller or project sponsor if the buyer defaults on financial or contractual obligations. 备用信用证
Reinsurance Facility A risk transfer structure where primary insurers or ECAs cede a portion of their credit portfolio risk to commercial reinsurers or multilateral development banks. 再保险工具 / 再保险安排
