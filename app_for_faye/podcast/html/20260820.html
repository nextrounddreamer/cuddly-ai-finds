Bilingual Concept Guide / 双语核心概念指南

English Concept Chinese Concept (中文) English Explanation Chinese Explanation (中文解释)
Co-Guarantee Mechanism 联合担保机制 A risk-sharing arrangement where two or more ECAs jointly back export credit debt, reducing single-agency concentration limits on large multi-sourced projects. 多家出口信用机构共同承担出口信贷债务风险的机制，旨在降低单一机构对大型多国采购项目的额度集中度限制。
Political Risk Insurance (PRI) 政治风险保险 Specialized insurance covering equity, debt, or assets against non-commercial risks such as currency inconvertibility, expropriation, war, and sovereign contract breach. 针对外汇管制、征收、战争及主权违约等非商业风险，保护股权、债权或资产安全的专项保险。
Non-Recourse Project Finance 无追索权项目融资 A financing structure where lenders are repaid solely from project cash flows, with no financial recourse to the parent balance sheet of project sponsors. 借款人的偿债来源仅限于项目自身的现金流与资产，对发起人母公司资产无追索权的融资结构。
ESG-Linked Supply Chain Finance ESG关联供应链融资 Supply chain financing platforms that adjust supplier discount rates based on verified environmental, social, and governance compliance metrics. 根据供应商经核实的ESG（环境、社会与治理）合规表现，动态调整贴现利率的供应链融资平台。
First-Loss Guarantee 优先受偿担保 / 第一损失担保 A credit enhancement instrument where the provider agrees to absorb initial project losses up to a defined limit before other syndicate lenders incur losses. 一种信用增级工具，担保方同意在特定限额内优先吸收项目最初发生的损失，从而保护银团其他出资方。
