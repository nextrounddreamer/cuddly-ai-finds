Bilingual Concept Guide / 双语核心概念指南

English Concept Chinese Concept (中文) English Explanation Chinese Explanation (中文解释)
Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) Off-take Guarantee 可持续航空燃料 (SAF) 包销担保 A credit protection mechanism issued by ECAs to back commercial project debt for synthetic jet fuel facilities, tied to long-term purchase agreements with international airlines. 由出口信用机构出具的信用保护机制，旨在为合成航空燃料设施的商业项目债务提供担保，并与国际航空公司签署的长期采购协议挂钩。
ESG KPI-Linked ECA Credit 绑定ESG关键绩效指标的出口信贷 Export financing structures where borrowing margins and guarantee fees adjust dynamically based on the project's adherence to pre-agreed decarbonization targets. 一种出口融资结构，其借款利差和担保费率根据项目是否达到预先约定的脱碳目标进行动态调整。
Cross-Border Energy Interconnector Facility 跨国能源互联线路融资工具 Structured loan cover supporting subsea cable infrastructure that links renewable energy generation across national borders to balance grid stability. 支持海底电缆基础设施建设的结构化贷款承保，用于连接跨国可再生能源发电网络，以平衡电网稳定性。
Political Risk Cover for Grid Infrastructure 电网基础设施政治风险保险 Insurance issued by sovereign ECAs to protect private commercial bank syndicates against government breach-of-contract, expropriation, or tariff currency controls in energy grid projects. 主权出口信用机构发行的保险，旨在保护私人商业银行银团免受能源电网项目中政府违约、征收或关税外汇管控等风险影响。
Reciprocal Risk-Sharing Framework 互惠风险分担框架 A bilateral agreement between two sovereign ECAs to automatically co-insure multi-sourced clean tech exports without requiring separate deal-by-deal negotiation. 两家主权出口信用机构之间的双边协议，旨在无需逐笔单独谈判的情况下，自动为多国采购的清洁技术出口提供联合保险。
