Off-Grid Distributed Solar Guarantee 离网分布式太阳能担保 Sovereign ECA credit protection covering debt facilities for decentralized solar microgrid installations and energy storage systems in rural and developing regions. 主权出口信用机构提供的信用保护，涵盖偏远及新兴地区分布式太阳能微电网设施和储能系统的债务融资。

Synthetic ECA Loan Securitization 合成出口信贷贷款证券化 The practice of transferring credit risk on ECA-covered loan portfolios to institutional capital markets using credit default swaps or financial guarantees. 通过信用违约互换或财务担保将具备出口信用机构出具的贷款组合信用风险转移至机构资本市场的实践。
Nordic Reciprocal Energy Guarantee 北欧互惠能源担保 A cross-border risk-sharing agreement between Scandinavian sovereign ECAs designed to co-underwrite complex offshore grid and renewable generation projects. 北欧主权出口信用机构之间的跨国风险分担协议，旨在共同承保复杂的海上电网及可再生能源发电项目。
Synthetic Capital Relief Tranche 合成资本缓释分层 A structured credit tranche engineered to absorb initial loan portfolio losses, enabling banks to reduce Risk-Weighted Assets under Basel III rules. 旨在吸收初始贷款组合损失的结构化信贷分层，帮助银行在巴塞尔协议III规则下有效降低风险加权资产。
