Bilingual Concept Guide / 双语核心概念指南

English Concept Chinese Concept (中文) English Explanation Chinese Explanation (中文解释)
Clean Ammonia Supply Chain 清洁氨供应链 An integrated production, transport, and storage network for zero-carbon or low-carbon ammonia, utilized as a hydrogen carrier and clean fuel for power generation and maritime transport. 零碳或低碳氨的生产、运输与储存一体化网络，用作氢能载体及用于电力与海运领域的清洁燃料。
Trade Asset Tokenization 贸易资产代币化 The creation of digital cryptographic tokens representing fractional ownership or rights in underlying trade finance receivables, bills of exchange, or letters of credit. 创建代表底层贸易融资应收账款、汇票或信用证部分所有权或权益的数字加密代币的过程。
Short-Term Trade Credit Insurance (ST Credit) 短期贸易信用保险 Credit insurance coverage typically under 360 days, designed to protect exporters against buyer insolvency or non-payment risks in commercial product shipments. 期限通常在360天以内的信用保险，旨在保护出口商免受商业货物运输中的买方破产或拖欠风险。
Regulatory Capital Relief 监管资本缓解 The reduction in required capital reserves a bank must maintain against loan assets, achieved through ECA guarantees, credit insurance, or recognized credit risk mitigation. 通过出口信用机构担保、信用保险或合规的信用风险缓释手段，降低银行就贷款资产必须持有的监管资本金要求。
Subordinated Debt Facility 次级债务工具 / 夹层融资 A junior debt instrument that ranks below senior debt in claims on assets or earnings, frequently used in ECA blended structures to absorb early risk. 在资产或收益求偿权上低于高级债务的次级债务工具，常用于出口信用机构混合融资结构中以吸收初期风险。
