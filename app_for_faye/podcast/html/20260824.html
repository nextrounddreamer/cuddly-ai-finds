Bilingual Concept Guide / 双语核心概念指南

English Concept Chinese Concept (中文) English Explanation Chinese Explanation (中文解释)
Synthetic Securitization / Portfolio Risk Transfer (PRT) 合成证券化 / 投资组合风险转移 A risk mitigation structure where a bank uses credit default swaps or financial guarantees to transfer credit risk on a loan portfolio to institutional investors without transferring underlying assets. 银行通过信用违约互换或金融担保将贷款组合的信用风险转移给机构投资者，而无需转移底层资产的风险缓释结构。
E-Methanol & Synthetic Green Fuels 电子甲醇与合成绿色燃料 Net-zero carbon fuels produced by combining captured biogenic carbon dioxide with green hydrogen, used to decarbonize maritime shipping and heavy industrial sectors. 通过捕集的生物质二氧化碳与绿色氢气合成的净零碳燃料，用于远洋航运及重工业领域的脱碳。
Small Modular Reactor (SMR) Financing 小型模块化反应堆 (SMR) 融资 Specialized export credit and project debt structures tailored for factory-assembled, lower-capital-cost nuclear power units delivering clean baseload electricity. 为工厂预制、资本成本较低的小型核电单元量身定制的出口信贷与项目债务结构，用于提供清洁基荷电力。
First-Loss Tranche Protection 优先损失分级保护 A credit enhancement layer in securitization where a designated guarantor or investor absorbs the initial credit losses of a portfolio up to a specified cap. 证券化中的信用增级层，指定担保人或投资者在特定上限内优先吸收资产组合的最初信用损失。
Grid Interconnection Capacity 电网互联容量 The maximum physical power transfer capability between separate national or regional electrical grids, critical for balancing intermittent renewable energy flows. 不同国家或区域电网之间物理输电的最大能力，对于平抑间歇性可再生能源波动至关重要。
