EKN & Finnvera Unveil $2.9B Co-Guarantee for North Baltic Subsea Interconnectors

The Swedish Export Credit Agency (EKN) and Finland's Finnvera executed a reciprocal co-underwriting framework backing long-tenor commercial bank loans for offshore wind integration and subsea HVDC power cables across the Northern Baltic Sea.

Bilingual Concept Guide / 双语核心概念指南

English Concept Chinese Concept (中文) English Explanation Chinese Explanation (中文解释)
Hydrometallurgical Recycling Guarantee 湿法冶金回收出口担保 A specialized credit insurance mechanism backing capital investments in closed-loop chemical extraction facilities that recover critical battery minerals like lithium and cobalt. 一种专项信用保险机制，旨在为回收锂和钴等关键电池矿物的闭环化学萃取设施资本投资提供担保。
Subsea HVDC Cable Co-Underwriting 海底高压直流电缆联合承保 A bilateral risk-sharing agreement between national ECAs to insure commercial project debt for high-voltage direct current power lines linking cross-border offshore renewables. 国家级出口信用机构之间的双边风险分担协议，用于对连接跨国海上可再生能源的高压直流电力线路的商业项目债务进行保险。
Zero-Emission Transit Fleet Cover 零排放交通车队承保机制 Sovereign ECA credit protection shielding commercial bank lenders against municipal default or political breach in urban electric and hydrogen public transit projects. 主权出口信用机构提供的信用保护，保护商业银行贷款人免受城市电动及氢能公共交通项目中的市政违约或政治风险影响。
Tokenized ECA Receivable Securitization 代币化出口信贷应收账款证券化 The conversion of ECA-backed trade debt into digital ledger tokens, facilitating secondary distribution to institutional investors while maintaining Credit Risk Mitigation benefits. 将具备出口信用机构担保的贸易债务转化为数字账本代币的过程，在保留信用风险缓释效益的同时促进二级市场机构投资者分销。
Closed-Loop Battery Metal Off-take 闭环电池金属包销协议 A commercial agreement binding clean tech manufacturers to purchase recycled battery-grade materials, backed by ECA untied guarantees to secure long-term project financing. 约束清洁技术制造商采购回收电池级材料的商业协议，通常辅以出口信用机构非绑定担保以获取长期项目融资。
