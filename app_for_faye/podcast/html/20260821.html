Bilingual Concept Guide / 双语核心概念指南

English Concept Chinese Concept (中文) English Explanation Chinese Explanation (中文解释)
Bilateral Reinsurance Structure 双边再保险结构 An arrangement where a lead ECA insures the entire transaction while obtaining backing from a secondary ECA for goods sourced in the partner country, reducing administrative friction. 主导出口信用机构对整笔交易提供承保，同时从合作国机构获取该国采购部分的再保险支持，从而简化多国采购的监管手续。
High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) Interconnector 高压直流 (HVDC) 联网线路 Long-distance power transmission infrastructure enabling cross-border trading of renewable energy with minimal line loss between distinct regional electricity grids. 用于远距离输电的基础设施，可在不同区域电网之间实现可再生能源的低损耗跨境交易与调配。
Digital Standards Initiative (DSI) 数字标准倡议 (DSI) An ICC-led framework establishing interoperable, paperless digital standards for trade documentation, electronic bills of lading, and automated credit verification. 由国际商会主导的倡议，旨在为贸易单证、电子提单及自动化信用审核建立跨平台互操作的无纸化数字标准。
Credit Risk Mitigation (CRM) 信用风险缓释 (CRM) Techniques such as guarantees, credit insurance, and collateral utilized by financial institutions to reduce potential credit loss and lower regulatory capital requirements under Basel guidelines. 金融机构利用担保、信用保险及抵押品等手段降低潜在信用损失，并在巴塞尔协议框架下减少监管资本计提的风险管理措施。
Untied Infrastructure Cover 非绑定基础设施承保 Export credit insurance or guarantees issued to finance critical public infrastructure without imposing strict national equipment procurement quotas on project sponsors. 为关键公共基础设施项目提供融资支持的出口信用保险或担保，不对项目发起人设立严格的本国设备采购比例限制。
