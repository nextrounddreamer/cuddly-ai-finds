Bilingual Concept Guide / 双语核心概念指南

English Concept Chinese Concept (中文) English Explanation Chinese Explanation (中文解释)
Untied Loan Guarantee (ULG) 无绑定贷款担保 A financial guarantee provided by an ECA to lenders that does not mandate direct procurement from the ECA's home country, often targeting strategic supply chain security or clean energy imports. 由出口信用机构向贷款人提供的金融担保，不强制要求从担保机构母国直接采购，通常用于保障战略供应链安全或清洁能源进口。
Standby Letter of Credit (SBLC) 备用信用证 A legal commitment issued by a bank guaranteeing payment to a beneficiary if the applicant defaults, widely used in trade finance and project performance guarantees. 银行出具的法定承诺，保证在申请人违约时向受益人履行付款责任，广泛应用于贸易融资及项目履约担保。
Blended Transition Finance 混合转型融资 The strategic use of concessional capital from development institutions alongside ECA guarantees to crowd in commercial bank syndicate liquidity for green projects. 战略性地结合来自发展机构的优惠资金与出口信用机构担保，旨在吸引商业银行银团资金进入绿色转型项目。
Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA) 风险加权资产 A regulatory measure of bank assets calculated according to their exposure risk, dictating capital adequacy requirements under Basel guidelines. 根据敞口风险计算的银行资产监管指标，直接决定巴塞尔协议框架下银行的资本充足率要求。
Chinese "New Three" Green Exports 中国“新三样”绿色出口 Refers to China's core high-tech green export drivers: Electric Vehicles (EVs), Lithium Batteries, and Photovoltaic (PV) solar products. 指中国核心的高新技术绿色出口“新三样”引擎：电动汽车、锂电池和光伏产品。
