Bilingual Concept Guide / 双语核心概念

English Concept English Explanation 中文解析
Political Risk Insurance (PRI) Insurance cover issued by ECAs or private insurers protecting investors and lenders against non-commercial risks such as expropriation, currency inconvertibility, and political violence. 政治风险保险 (PRI)
Untied Sovereign Guarantee A state-backed credit guarantee that secures project debt without restricting equipment procurement to the issuing country, often used for critical commodity security. 无绑定主权担保
Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA) A banking regulatory measure used to determine the minimum amount of capital that banks must hold as a cushion against unexpected losses in trade and credit portfolios. 风险加权资产 (RWA)
Transaction-Level Underwriting Real-time, granular credit and operational risk assessment focusing on specific trade shipments rather than static annual corporate balance sheets. 交易级承销 / 实时交易审核
Curtailed Energy Protection Specialized insurance or guarantee clauses covering renewable power producers against losses incurred when grid operators restrict electricity feed-in. 弃电/限电风险补偿机制
