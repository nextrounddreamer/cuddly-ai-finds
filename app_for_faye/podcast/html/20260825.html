Bilingual Concept Guide / 双语核心概念指南

English Concept Chinese Concept (中文) English Explanation Chinese Explanation (中文解释)
Dual-Agency Co-Underwriting 双机构联合承保 A risk-sharing structure where two national ECAs issue joint export credit guarantees under standardized documentation, reducing counterparty friction in multi-sourced manufacturing projects. 两家国家级出口信用机构在标准化文件下共同出具出口信贷担保的风险分担结构，旨在减少多国采购制造项目中的交易壁垒。
Gigafactory Supply Chain Guarantee 超级工厂供应链担保 Targeted credit cover designed for high-capacity battery manufacturing facilities, covering capital equipment procurement, technology transfers, and raw material inputs. 针对大容量电池制造工厂的专项信用承保，涵盖资本性设备采购、技术转让及原材料供应。
Untied Rare Earth Guarantee 稀土元素非绑定担保 Sovereign ECA guarantee backing project debt for critical mineral extraction without restricting procurement strictly to domestic exporters, securing strategic material off-takes. 主导出口信用机构为关键矿产开采项目债务提供的担保，不严格限制采购自本国出口商，旨在保障战略性原材料的包销权。
Commercial ECA Syndication 商业性出口信贷银团分销 The practice of primary lending banks selling down ECA-backed debt tranches to secondary institutional investors while maintaining regulatory capital mitigation benefits. 主牵头行将具有出口信用机构担保的债务分额分销给二级市场机构投资者，同时保留监管资本缓释效益的业务实践。
Critical Mineral Off-take Agreement 关键矿产包销协议 A long-term commercial contract binding a buyer to purchase future production of refined battery materials, often backed by ECA untied guarantees to secure project financing. 买方与生产方签署的长期商业合同，约定购买未来精炼电池材料的产出，通常辅以出口信用机构非绑定担保以帮助项目获取融资。
