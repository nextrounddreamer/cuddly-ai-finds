Blended Green Export Credit 混合型绿色出口信贷 A financial structuring model combining concessional multilateral funds, commercial debt, and sovereign ECA guarantees to reduce overall borrowing costs for green infrastructure. 一种金融结构化模式，结合多边优惠资金、商业债务和主权出口信用机构担保，以降低绿色基础设施的整体融资成本。
