Bilingual Concept Guide / 双语核心概念指南

English Concept Chinese Concept (中文) English Explanation Chinese Explanation (中文解释)
Model Law on Electronic Transferable Records (MLETR) 可转让电子记录示范法 A UNCITRAL legal framework enabling electronic transferable records (e.g., electronic bills of lading) to achieve legal functional equivalence with paper counterparts. 联合国国际贸易法委员会确立的法律框架，旨在赋予电子可转让单据（如电子提单）与纸质单据同等的法律效力。
Carbon Capture & Storage (CCS) Debt Guarantee 碳捕集与封存 (CCS) 债务担保 Specialized export insurance protecting commercial lenders against performance and credit default risks in industrial point-source carbon mitigation projects. 针对工业点源碳减排项目中的履约和信用违约风险，向商业贷款人提供的专项出口保险。
Subsea Interconnector Debt Structure 海底电网互联线路债务结构 Long-term structured credit facilities designed to finance high-voltage direct current (HVDC) subsea power cables linking offshore wind generation directly to cross-border power grids. 用于资助连接海上风电与跨国电网的高压直流 (HVDC) 海底电力缆线建设的长远期结构化信贷工具。
Climate Adaptation Blended Facility 气候适应型混合融资工具 A financing model combining public developmental capital, commercial bank debt, and sovereign ECA guarantees to fund climate-resilient water and coastal infrastructure. 结合公共开发资金、商业银行债务和主权出口信用机构担保的融资模式，用于资助具备气候韧性的水利及海岸基础设施。
Electronic Bill of Lading (eBL) Tokenization 电子提单代币化 The representation of digital trade title documents as cryptographic ledger tokens, facilitating real-time transferability, verification, and balance-sheet relief for lenders. 将数字贸易物权凭证表示为加密账本代币的过程，便于贷款人实现实时转让、验证及资产负债表资本缓释。
